## Before you start

The handbook is for students who know algebra and analysis at the level of a first graduate year, and for mathematicians who do not read Lean. Each chapter defines its objects, proves its results, and ends with exercises and notes. Each chapter also names a standard textbook to read with it.

Start with Volume 0 on every path. It explains how to read the handbook, gives the program in one chapter, and fixes the notation and the normalizations: the geometric Frobenius, the unitary or arithmetic Satake parameters, and the choice of additive character. The later volumes follow these choices.

## The paths

1. **Class field theory.** Read Volume 1 (number fields, local fields and adeles), then Volume 3 (class field theory and $\mathrm{GL}_1$). Volume 3 needs only the compact groups of Volume 2. This path covers the part of the program that is a full theorem, and the first part that the project proves in Lean.
2. **Modular forms and $\mathrm{GL}_2$.** Read Volume 1, then Volume 4 (modular forms and elliptic curves). Then read Volumes 2 and 3, and Volume 5 (automorphic representations of $\mathrm{GL}_2$).
3. **The local correspondence.** Read Volumes 2 and 3, then Volume 6 (representations of $p$-adic groups and the local Langlands correspondence). Volume 6 contains the local proof for $\mathrm{GL}_2$ after Bushnell-Henniart.
4. **The trace formula.** Read Volumes 5 and 6, then Volume 8 (the trace formula) and Volume 9 (endoscopy and the classification of Arthur). Volume 7 (L-functions, converse theorems and functoriality for $\mathrm{GL}_n$) helps for Volume 8.
5. **Galois representations and modularity.** Read Volumes 4, 5, 6 and 8, then Volume 10 (Shimura varieties and automorphic Galois representations) and Volume 11 (Galois deformations and modularity).
6. **The research frontier.** Volume 12 covers $p$-adic Hodge theory, $p$-adic Langlands and the geometrization of the local correspondence. Volume 13 covers function fields, geometric Langlands, and the program in 2026 with its open problems. Volume 13 needs Volumes 2, 3 and 6.
